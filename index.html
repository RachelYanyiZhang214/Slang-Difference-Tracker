How to interpret the project

These are pragmatic comparisons, not national personality tests. A phrase can reveal a recurring interactional pattern without describing every speaker.

01 / CONTROL

Same situation

Every language answers the same interactional question. This prevents the atlas from becoming a random collection of “interesting foreign words.”

02 / CONTEXT

Function before translation

The social job comes first: greet, encourage, reassure, recognize effort, react. Literal wording comes second.

03 / CAUTION

Pattern, not stereotype

Region, age, relationship, tone and setting all change language. Each card therefore includes a “do not overgeneralize” note.
